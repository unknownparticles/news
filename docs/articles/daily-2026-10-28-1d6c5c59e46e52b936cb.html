Ai2 研究发现 LLM 新颖性裁判对评估设置敏感

Ai2 发布 Old Ideas, Novel Problems 研究、基准和数据，测试模型判断研究想法新颖性的稳定性。作者在所测模型与设置中发现，提示、比较形式、推理及检索选择会明显改变判断；其中 Human+Generated 的部分标签基于假设，文章明确是弱标签，不是逐条已核实真值。研究不证明所有模型均同样不可靠，本期未复现。

为什么值得关注

科研辅助中的评价器本身也需要检验，引用检索和更多推理不能直接当作可靠性保证。

下一步

用公开想法对固定参考文献、提示和展示顺序做重复对照，检查结论翻转及证据，再由领域人员审阅。

  • Ai2 · 新颖性评估研究 ↗
    Our new benchmark results suggest novelty evaluation itself is far from reliable. Across six LLM judges – GPT-5.1, GPT-5.2, GPT-5.4, Claude Sonnet 4.5, Claude Opus 4.5, and Claude Opus 4.6 – seemingly small changes to the evaluation setup produce dramatically different judgments of the same ideas. In one experiment, changing just a few words in a prompt shifts a model’s accuracy in identifying which of two ideas is more novel by more than 50 percentage points. Retrieval and additional reasoning do not consistently improve judgments, and two purpose-built novelty evaluation systems perform worse than a simple prompted Claude Opus 4.6 baseline.
